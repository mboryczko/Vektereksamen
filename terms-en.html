Purpose of the app

The app is an independent revision aid for the Norwegian security guard exam. It is not official and is unaffiliated with the Norwegian Police, the Norwegian Police Directorate or any course provider. It contains practice questions written for the app, not the real exam questions.

It does not replace mandatory education, practical training or the official exam. Using the app or passing a mock exam provides no qualification or guarantee of an exam pass.

Content and limitations

Rules and guidance may change. Follow current regulations and updated information from public authorities and your course provider. Some questions are labelled “Allmenn kunnskap” and use general principles rather than a specific public source or instructor approval.

First aid, security, use of force and legal content is study material. It is not individual medical or legal advice or operational instructions for a specific incident. Please report content errors.

Free and full versions

The free version contains 28 selected questions, practice with those questions, a mini test and statistics. The full version unlocks the full question bank, full mock exams, mistake revision and exam history. Detailed review is kept for the latest 50 exams; older records may show summaries.

The full version is purchased once through the App Store. It is not a subscription. The App Store displays the current price before purchase. The purchase covers the product offered, without a promise of a particular number of future updates, unlimited support or compatibility with every future iOS version. Statutory update and conformity requirements continue to apply.

Purchases can be restored in Settings. Access may change after a refund or purchase revocation. Family Sharing depends on product availability in the App Store and Apple's terms. The purchase does not include syncing or recovering local progress.
