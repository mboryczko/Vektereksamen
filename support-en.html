Report a question error

Choose “Report an error in this question” below the explanation in study or exam review. Describe the problem in up to 500 characters, then tap “Send report”. The app automatically includes the question ID and question bank version and sends the report through TelemetryDeck. Do not include personal information. Confirmation means the report has been queued; delivery requires internet access. No email app is needed. For a personal reply or to share a source or screenshot, use the email address above.

What is free, and what is paid?

The free version includes 28 selected questions with explanations, practice using those questions, a 10-question mini test and statistics. The full version unlocks the complete question bank, full mock exams, Repeat mistakes and exam history with review of recent exams.

The full version is a one-time purchase with no subscription or automatic renewal. The App Store shows the current price and currency before you confirm. The purchase provides app access, not an exam booking or course enrolment.

I purchased, but the full version is locked

  1. Connect to the internet and check that the App Store uses the same Apple Account as the original purchase.
  2. Open Settings → Restore purchases in Vektereksamen.
  3. If it fails, close and reopen the app and try again. Check that your device allows in-app purchases.
  4. Contact us if access is still missing. Describe the error; hide unrelated purchases and personal information in any receipt screenshot.

Do not buy again to troubleshoot this. A pending purchase needs approval before access is granted. See Apple's restore purchase help.

Can I share the purchase with my family?

The full version is designed for Family Sharing. Availability depends on the product being enabled for sharing in the App Store and on Apple's rules and your family settings. The app does not share progress or results.

How do I request a refund?

For a purchase billed by Apple, submit a request at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple assesses requests under the applicable terms. Contact us about app features or faults. This does not limit your statutory consumer rights. See Apple's refund guide.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Practice, questions and explanations work offline. Purchases, restoration, external sources and analytics delivery require internet access. Purchase status is cached for offline use after access has been verified.

New phone or reinstalling

Restore your purchase with the same Apple Account. The app does not provide its own progress sync or server backup. Restoration through Apple's device backup depends on your backup and settings. Avoid deleting the app as the first troubleshooting step if you want to preserve local history.

How do I reset progress?

Choose Settings → Reset progress and confirm. This clears local learning records and exam history without affecting your purchase. See data deletion and privacy choices.

Language and the official exam

Choose Settings → App language. The interface supports Norwegian Bokmål and English. Questions, answers and explanations remain in Norwegian Bokmål.

This is an independent practice app, unaffiliated with the Norwegian Police, the Norwegian Police Directorate or any course provider. Questions are not the official exam questions. The app does not replace mandatory training or guarantee a pass. Consult the Norwegian Police and your course provider for official information. Mock exam settings change only the exercise in the app.
