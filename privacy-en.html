Vektereksamen privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who is responsible?

Michał Boryczko, Kraków, Poland, is responsible for Vektereksamen and the personal data processing described here. Contact: mb.software.studio@gmail.com.

Information on your device

The app requires no user account and contains no advertising. Answers, learning progress, exam results and exam history are stored locally on your device. Settings, language preferences and full version access status are also stored locally. There is no app-operated cloud sync for progress. Apple-managed device backups may include app data, depending on your settings.

We do not receive your answers or results through app analytics. The app does not request access to contacts, your camera, microphone or precise location.

Usage analytics with TelemetryDeck

We use TelemetryDeck to understand feature use and improve the app. The app sends events about launches and sessions, study, mock exams and the exam guide, viewing the purchase screen, and the outcome of purchase and restore attempts. Additional values describe study mode, why the purchase screen appeared and whether a purchase was found.

The SDK adds technical information including app and operating system version, device model, screen information, language, region, time zone, display and accessibility preferences, and session statistics. Region is a device preference, not GPS location. The SDK uses a hashed device-based identifier and a session identifier. We do not supply a custom user ID. See the SDK default parameters.

Our custom events do not contain question IDs, selected answers, scores, payment information, transaction IDs or error message contents. A failed purchase event reports the outcome without error details.

The provider is TelemetryDeck GmbH in Germany. The provider states that analytics identifiers undergo further anonymisation on its servers, IP addresses are not stored, and analytics servers are in the EU. We do not use this information for advertising tracking or advertising profiles. The provider does not guarantee a deletion deadline for archived analytics events. See its privacy FAQ.

TelemetryDeck describes the information as anonymous. We use the statistics to improve the product and do not attempt to identify individuals. The app currently has no dedicated analytics switch; resetting progress does not disable analytics.

Purchases through Apple

The full version is purchased through Apple. StoreKit verifies purchases and access on your device. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy policy. Restoring purchases restores access, not local learning history.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name provided, your message and attachments. The app's question report draft may include the question ID, question bank version and app version. No email is sent until you send it yourself.

We use this information to respond, investigate problems and handle purchase-related enquiries. Contract-related support relies on GDPR Article 6(1)(b). Other enquiries rely on Article 6(1)(f): our legitimate interest in answering voluntary enquiries and correcting problems. You do not need to send sensitive information, identity documents, passwords or card details.

Support email is handled in Google's Gmail. See Google's privacy policy. Google may process information outside the EEA. Google describes its use of adequacy decisions, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses where applicable in its international data transfer information.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as needed to resolve and follow up on the enquiry. When it is no longer needed, it should be deleted. Retention depends on whether the case remains open or requires follow-up. Where information is needed for a legal obligation or a specific dispute, only the necessary information is retained for as long as that purpose requires. Question corrections may be documented without your contact information.

Deletion and your rights

Use Settings → Reset progress to clear answers and exam history. Your purchase is unaffected. This does not delete settings, support emails, Apple's purchase records or previously transmitted analytics. See data deletion and privacy choices.

For personal data we process, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction and, where applicable, portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Email the address above; we normally respond within one month. We cannot link anonymised analytics events to your email address. More about these rights.

You may complain to a supervisory authority where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred, including Norway's Datatilsynet or Poland's UODO.

These web pages

The documentation pages are hosted on GitHub Pages at mboryczko.github.io/Vektereksamen/. We have not added web analytics, advertising, cookies, forms or remotely loaded fonts to these pages. GitHub nevertheless processes technical information to deliver and secure its service and states that visitors' IP addresses are logged for security purposes. We do not maintain a separate visitor log for these pages. GitHub describes retention, transfers and rights in its privacy statement. See also GitHub Pages data collection.

External links and changes

External sources, Apple and other websites have their own privacy rules. This policy also covers the documentation pages described below. We update the date above when this policy changes and provide an in-app notice for material changes to data processing.
