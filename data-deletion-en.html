Data deletion and privacy choices

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Vektereksamen has no app account to delete.

Local progress

Open Settings → Reset progress → Reset. This removes recorded answers, revision data, results and exam history from the app's active local storage. There is no undo in the app. Your full version purchase is unaffected.

Resetting does not delete language preferences, other settings or purchase status. To remove the app's local files and settings, use Delete App in iOS. Removing the icon from the Home Screen or offloading the app is different from deleting app data. Device backups may still contain earlier app data; manage these separately in Apple's settings.

Support correspondence

Email mb.software.studio@gmail.com from the address you used to contact us and identify the conversation concerned. You do not need to send an identity document initially. If information must be kept for legal reasons, we will explain what and why. See retention and rights in the privacy policy.

Analytics events

Resetting progress or deleting the app does not delete events already sent to TelemetryDeck. We have no link between an email address and anonymised analytics events, so we cannot promise to locate or delete those events for an individual.

The current app has no analytics switch in Settings. Resetting progress does not disable analytics. The SDK may deliver queued events when you reopen the app with internet access. Contact us with analytics questions.

Apple's purchase records

Resetting the app does not erase purchases or information held by Apple. Contact Apple about personal information in your Apple Account or refunds. See Apple's privacy service and purchase problem reporting.
